TechInnovate Mobility — Delete My Account

Applies to Driver and Owner accounts on the TechInnovate Mobility app.

You can request permanent deletion of your TechInnovate Mobility account and login credentials in two ways — directly in the app, or from this page.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Drivers: Log in → tap My Logs in the bottom bar → scroll to Account → Delete My Account → type DELETE to confirm.
  2. Owners: Log in → open the sidebar menu → Delete My Account → type DELETE to confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion from this page

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from your registered email address with the subject line "Delete my account", including your registered mobile number or email.

Email us to request deletion

We process manual requests within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number, email address
  • Your login PIN (drivers) or password (owners)
  • Your ability to log in — immediately and permanently

What is retained (and why)

Fuel fill records, trip logs, and fleet financial entries linked to your account are anonymized (your name is replaced with "Deleted Driver" / "Deleted Owner") but the underlying records stay in the fleet owner's history. This is required for:

  • Fleet accounting and CNG credit reconciliation
  • Tax and financial record-keeping (up to 7 years, per Indian business record requirements)
  • Fraud prevention and dispute resolution

See our Privacy Policy, Sections 5 and 8, for full details.
