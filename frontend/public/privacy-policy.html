TechInnovate Mobility — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 July 2026  ·  App: TechInnovate Mobility (com.techinnovate.mobility.cng)

TechInnovate Mobility ("we", "our", "us") operates the TechInnovate Mobility mobile application — a fleet CNG monitoring, trip tracking, and load-matching platform for tempo owners and drivers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, and login PIN (drivers) or email and password (owners).
  • Business details (owners): business name, fleet vehicle details (plate numbers, models, odometer readings), driver assignments.
  • Fuel fill records: CNG quantity, price, station name, receipts, and odometer photos you capture.
  • Load and tempo listings: route, cargo, vehicle, and rate information you post or book.

1.2 Information collected with your permission

PermissionWhy we need it
Location (GPS), including background locationLive trip tracking for fleet owners, recording fill-up and trip start/end locations, and navigation assistance during active trips.
Camera (rear camera, photo and video)Capturing odometer readings, fuel receipts, and vehicle condition photos/videos for fill verification.

You can decline any permission; related features will be unavailable, but the rest of the app keeps working. You can withdraw permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → TechInnovate Mobility → Permissions.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device information (model, OS version, app version) for troubleshooting.
  • Usage data (features used, sync timestamps) to improve the product.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate the app: record fuel fills, track trips, manage fleets, match loads.
  • Show fleet owners the live location and trip status of drivers assigned to their fleet during active trips.
  • Verify fill records with photos and GPS to prevent fraud.
  • Calculate driver wallet rewards and owner credit usage.
  • Send service notifications (fill approvals, payout status, load bookings).
  • Verify your identity via email/SMS OTP.
  • Improve app performance and fix defects.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. Location Data — Important Notes

  • Driver location is shared only with the fleet owner the driver is assigned to, and only in the context of trips and fill records.
  • Background location is used only while a trip is active so that owners can track deliveries in progress.
  • Location capture stops when the trip ends or you log out.

4. Sharing of Information

  • Within your fleet: owners see their drivers' fills, trips, and locations; drivers see loads and listings posted for them.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, database, and email/SMS delivery vendors, bound by confidentiality, used only to run the service.
  • Legal: if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and prevent fraud.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Fill records, trip logs, and verification media are retained for up to 7 years for business and taxation records, then deleted or anonymised.
  • You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time (see Section 8).

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), authenticated with tokens, and access to owner financial data is restricted by role. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take commercially reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for commercial vehicle owners and drivers aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or update your account information in the app.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal data by emailing us.
  • Withdraw device permissions at any time via Android Settings.
  • Delete your account: email us with the subject "Delete my account" from your registered email.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in the app. The "Effective date" above always reflects the latest version.
